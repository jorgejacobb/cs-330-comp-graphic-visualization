# CS-330 Journal

![3D Scene](images/scene.png)

Throughout this project, I approached designing software as both a **technical and creative process**. Creative thinking in this class was indispensable, because building a 3D scene required more than just writing functional code. It required intentional decisions about **spatial composition, lighting, color palettes, texture usage, and user perspective**. My design process followed a clear cycle of **thinking, designing, implementing, and refining**. I began by conceptualizing the environment and defining its purpose, then translated those ideas into **object placement, transformations, materials, and lighting configurations in OpenGL**. One of the most valuable design skills I developed was the ability to **balance aesthetics with technical constraints, ensuring that shaders, camera positioning, and object scaling worked together cohesively**. **Iterative refinement** played a critical role, as each milestone allowed me to evaluate visual results, incorporate instructor feedback, and improve **depth, realism, and scene structure**. These tactics, especially **iterative prototyping and feedback integration**, are strategies I will continue applying in future academic and professional projects.

In terms of development, I approached programming methodically, breaking the 3D scene into **modular components such as scene management, view management, textures, and lighting**. New development strategies included more structured debugging, incremental testing of transformations, and careful validation of rendering outputs after each adjustment. Iteration was central to my growth throughout the milestones. Early versions of my scene focused on functionality, while later versions emphasized polish, realism, and optimization. My coding approach evolved from simply making features work to intentionally designing **clean, maintainable, and well-structured implementations**. Computer science continues to help me reach my goals by strengthening both my **problem-solving mindset and my ability to transform abstract ideas into tangible systems**. Computational graphics and visualization, in particular, expanded my understanding of how **mathematics, logic, and creativity intersect**. These skills support my future educational pathway by deepening my **technical foundation in algorithms and system design**, and they strengthen my professional pathway by enhancing my ability to build **visually engaging, user-centered applications** that combine engineering precision with creative expression.
